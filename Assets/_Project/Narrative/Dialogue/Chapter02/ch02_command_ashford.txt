[if flag sided_with_radar] ASHFORD: Mara tells me you backed the radar schedule. I appreciate that.
[if flag hana_suspicious] ASHFORD: Hana came by. Asked some questions about the deep returns. Interesting questions.

  ~ trust MARA +1
  ~ trust ASHFORD -1

=== directive7
