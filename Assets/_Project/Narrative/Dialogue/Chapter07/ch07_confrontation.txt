# Chapter 7, scene 7.1. The drill chamber. Sam and Mara confront Ashford while Hana runs the drill under his orders.
# How it opens depends on what Sam chose after reading the sealed orders (ch06_sealed_orders_mara).
# It ends when the drill seizes: drill_seized starts the eruption (EruptionSequence).

=== start
[if flag confront_ashford] -> attack
[if flag warn_hana_first] -> hana_first
-> arrive

=== attack
MARA: Directive Seven, Daniel. A biological sample. Say it to our faces.
-> ashford

=== hana_first
SAM: Hana. Step away from the console. Please.
HANA: Sam? What's wrong?
MARA: Ask him what you're drilling for.
-> ashford

=== arrive
ASHFORD: Sam. Mara. The sweep is done already?
MARA: We read your orders.
-> ashford

=== ashford
ASHFORD: (long beat) Those orders were sealed for a reason.
HANA: Daniel? What is she talking about?
[if flag demanded_truth] ASHFORD: I promised you'd hear it from me, Sam. I meant it. I just meant later.
[if flag defended_ashford] MARA: Okafor thinks you had a reason. Go on. Give him one.
ASHFORD: Tiamat heard something under this ice. Our job is to find out what. That's all this is.
*! "Then why is Hana collecting a sample to take home?" -> sample
  ~ flag accused_ashford
  ~ trust ASHFORD -1
  ~ trust HANA +1
*! "Hana, stop the drill. Now." -> stop
  ~ flag ordered_drill_stop
  ~ trust HANA +1
* "Commander, we deserved to know." -> deserved
  ~ trust ASHFORD +1
* "(Look at the bore.)" -> look
  ~ flag noticed_bore_frost

=== sample
HANA: (beat) Daniel. Is that true?
ASHFORD: It was never going to hurt anyone.
-> seize

=== stop
HANA: I... Commander?
ASHFORD: Keep drilling, Doctor. That's an order.
-> seize

=== deserved
ASHFORD: (beat) Yes. You did. (beat) I'm sorry, Sam.
-> seize

=== look
SAM: The frost on the railing. It's growing. Toward us.
-> seize

=== seize
HANA: Wait. The torque is spiking. Something is holding the bit.
HANA: It's not ice. It's pulling back.
MARA: Kill the drill, Hana! Kill it!
~ flag drill_seized
-> END
