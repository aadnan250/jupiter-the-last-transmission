# Chapter 6, scene 6.2. The comms console in Conamara Base: eleven months of delayed mail from Earth.
# Tomas Brandt recorded one message for Sam. Whether Sam shows it to Mara is the chapter's choice:
# it decides how she opens the sealed-orders conversation (ch06_sealed_orders_mara) and her trust in Chapter 8.
# If Sam told her the truth on the ship (Chapter 2), she already knows, and asks to hear it anyway.

=== start
MARA: Mail cache is synced. Eleven months of it. Most of it is yours. Your daughter draws a lot.
MARA: (beat) There's one from Tomas. Addressed to you. Not me.
[if flag told_mara_truth_early] -> knows
* "(Put on the headset and play it.)" -> play
  ~ flag played_tomas_privately
* "Do you want to hear it first?" -> offer
  ~ trust MARA +1

=== offer
MARA: It's addressed to you, Okafor. Play it. I'll pretend I'm not watching.
-> play

=== play
~ flag heard_tomas_message
TOMAS (radio): Sam. (beat) If she still hates you by Jupiter, that's my fault.
TOMAS (radio): I asked you not to tell her about my heart, because I didn't want her flying angry at the agency.
TOMAS (radio): Turns out she's flying angry at you. (laughs) Sorry.
TOMAS (radio): Tell her it wasn't him. Tell her I said it.
MARA: Well?
*! "(Turn the screen toward her.)" -> show
  ~ flag showed_mara_message
  ~ trust MARA +2
*! "(Close it. It wasn't yours to hear.)" -> keep
  ~ flag kept_tomas_message
* "Just an old friend wishing me luck." -> lie
  ~ flag kept_tomas_message
  ~ flag lied_to_mara
  ~ trust MARA -2

=== show
MARA: (long beat) Why didn't you just say?
SAM: He asked me not to.
MARA: (beat) Idiot. (beat) Both of you.
MARA: Come on. Ashford's office is next door, and he isn't in it.
-> END

=== keep
MARA: (beat) Fine. Keep your secrets. Everyone else here does.
-> END

=== lie
MARA: (beat) Sure he was.
-> END
