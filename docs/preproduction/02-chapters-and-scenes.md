# 02 — Chapter and Scene Breakdown

Target runtime **~61 minutes** for a first-time player. Durations are estimates for planning, not guarantees.

**Control states:** **FP** = full first-person control · **FP-L** = limited first-person (walk only / look only, e.g., during dialogue) · **CS** = non-interactive cutscene · **QTE** is not used anywhere; every physical moment is played, not prompted.

---

## 1. Chapter overview

| # | Chapter | Location(s) | Duration | Emotional target | Core gameplay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goodbye, Earth | Launch facility, crew quarters, family room, gantry, launch vehicle cockpit | 6 min | Attachment, significance | Exploration, dialogue, launch checklist |
| 2 | The Journey | *Meridian* habitat ring | 7 min | Belonging, warmth | Exploration, conversations, small repairs, one choice |
| 3 | Jupiter | Observation deck, science bay | 5 min | Awe → first unease | Observation, sensor operation |
| 4 | Signal Loss | *Meridian* comms deck, spine corridor, antenna EVA hatch | 6 min | Uncertainty, wrongness | Power/comms puzzle, short tension sequence |
| 5 | Europa | Lander descent, surface EVA, Conamara Base exterior | 8 min | Beauty → discomfort | Surface exploration, radiation timer, environmental clues |
| 6 | The Discovery | Conamara Base interior, *Tiamat* site, drill head | 7 min | Suspicion, betrayal | Investigation, terminals, documents, choice |
| 7 | Contact | Drill chamber, habitat, lander pad | 8 min | Terror, chaos | First encounter, retreat, stealth, combat, radio deaths |
| 8 | The Last Crew Member | Service tunnels, drill chamber | 6 min | Fragile hope → devastation | Escort/co-op traversal, boss fight, death scene |
| 9 | The Last Transmission | Surface escape, ascent vehicle, *Meridian* | 5 min | Emptiness, resolve | Solo escape (the Keel), final choice, transmission |
| 10 | Home | Pacific recovery ship, Earth | 3 min | Relief mixed with grief | Walk, quiet interactions, credits |

## 2. Scene-by-scene breakdown

### Chapter 1 — Goodbye, Earth (6 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 Cold open | Black screen, a recorded transmission crackles: Sam's voice, exhausted, "This is Okafor, *Meridian*... if anyone—" cut. Title card. | CS | 0:30 |
| 1.2 Crew quarters, 06:10 | Sam wakes, room at a coastal launch facility (fictional **Cabo Lumen Spaceport**, Atlantic coast). Window shows the launch vehicle at dawn. Pack personal items: Zara's drawing is the interaction tutorial. | FP | 1:00 |
| 1.3 Family room | Ada and Zara. Short, warm, awkward goodbye. Zara asks whether Jupiter "has a face". Through the glass: other families. Mara stands alone at the far window. | FP-L | 1:30 |
| 1.4 Suit-up | Crew introductions while suiting. Mara fixes Sam's seal without being asked. Ashford's speech is short and human. Luca imitates him behind his back. | FP-L | 1:00 |
| 1.5 Gantry walk | Long walk across the gantry. Wind, birds, ocean. The last natural sounds the player will hear for a long time. | FP (walk) | 0:40 |
| 1.6 Launch | In-cockpit launch, real checklist callouts with CAPCOM, then the **exterior cinematic** (one shot) of the vehicle climbing into morning light. | FP-L → CS | 1:00 |
| 1.7 First view of Earth | Engines cut. Silence. Pencils float. Sam turns his head: Earth fills the window. Zara's drawing floats past. | FP-L (look) | 0:30 |

### Chapter 2 — The Journey (7 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 Docking (time card: "Day 3") | Brief: launch vehicle docks with the *Meridian* in Earth orbit. Interior establishing shot of the rotating ring. | CS | 0:30 |
| 2.2 "Month 4" — the ring | Free roam: galley (Luca's music), med bay (Hana), command (Ashford), workshop (Mara). Optional conversations reveal lives outside the mission. | FP | 2:30 |
| 2.3 Radar argument | Ashford schedules extra radar calibration; Mara needs the power budget for lander tests. Sam is caught between them. | FP-L | 0:45 |
| 2.4 The music player | Optional repair task in the workshop. Leave it on her bunk or not (minor choice, affects one later line). | FP | 0:45 |
| 2.5 EVA drill | Airlock drill in the ring; Sam slips, Mara catches him. "That's one, Okafor." | FP | 1:00 |
| 2.6 Earth message | A delayed video from Ada and Zara (43-min delay explained naturally). Sam records a reply. | FP-L | 1:00 |
| 2.7 Mars | Optional: from the observation deck, Mars as a bright reddish point (date-dependent; see overview §8). | FP | 0:30 |

### Chapter 3 — Jupiter (5 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 "Month 11" | Crew gathers. Ashford opens the observation shutters. | FP-L | 0:30 |
| 3.2 **The first full view** | Jupiter fills two-thirds of the window. Cloud bands drift; the Great Red Spot rotates into view. Io crosses in front of it, casting a small black shadow on the clouds. Players can look freely; no dialogue for 20 seconds. | FP-L (look) | 1:30 |
| 3.3 Science bay | Sam brings *Tiamat*'s old surface seismometers back online, which is routine. Data is sonified to the speakers for the crew's amusement ("Let's hear Europa breathe"). | FP | 1:00 |
| 3.4 **The first sound** | A deep, slow, rhythmic vibration through every speaker. Not a roar; a pressure in the room. Coffee rings in the mug. Crew reactions. Ashford goes quiet and very still (the attentive player notices). | FP-L | 1:00 |
| 3.5 Debrief | Ashford calls it a calibration artefact and moves on too quickly. | FP-L | 1:00 |

### Chapter 4 — Signal Loss (6 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 Radar pass | Sam runs the first orbital radar sounding of Conamara. Returns come back... reshaped. The pattern of his own pulses, slowed, returned with a delay. | FP | 1:00 |
| 4.2 Blackout | Comms fail; monitors flicker; ring lights drop to emergency red. A low hum in the hull. Luca's voice over the radio from the bridge, calm but too fast. | FP | 0:45 |
| 4.3 Restore comms (puzzle) | Reroute power at the junction, find the faulty relay (one of three, deduced from readouts), then realign the antenna from the spine hatch with Mara on the radio. "That's two." | FP | 3:00 |
| 4.4 Restored, but wrong | Comms back. A recorded packet from Grace at Mission Control arrives, then a second copy, which she never sent: the same message, slower, lower, with something underneath it. | FP-L | 1:15 |

### Chapter 5 — Europa (8 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 Descent | Lander *Wren* descends to Conamara Chaos. Jupiter hangs huge on the horizon. Luca flies; Mara calls out systems. | FP-L | 1:00 |
| 5.2 First steps | Low gravity (0.134 g). No wind, no sound outside the suit, only breathing and radio. Beauty: ice blocks the size of buildings, ridges, Jupiter fixed and unmoving. | FP | 1:30 |
| 5.3 Deploy instruments | Plant three seismic sensors along a crack. Radiation dosimeter on the HUD becomes a soft timer. | FP | 2:00 |
| 5.4 Wrong things | A sensor from an earlier cargo landing has been moved 40 m. It's wrapped in frost that shouldn't form in this vacuum (Rime). A ridge on the horizon wasn't on the survey maps. | FP | 1:30 |
| 5.5 The distant shape | A tall, thin shape on a far ridge, against Jupiter's light. Players who use the helmet zoom see it more clearly. When Sam looks back after a radio call, it's gone. | FP | 1:00 |
| 5.6 Conamara Base | Enter the habitat. Air pressure returns: **sound comes back** with a rush. Relief. | FP | 0:30 |

### Chapter 6 — The Discovery (7 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 Base exploration | Habitat systems check. Mail cache from Earth (delayed messages for everyone). | FP | 1:30 |
| 6.2 Tomas's message **[CHOICE]** | Sam finds Tomas Brandt's message: "Tell her it wasn't him." Show Mara, or keep the promise. | FP-L | 1:00 |
| 6.3 *Tiamat* site | Short EVA to the old lander. Borehole sealed *from below* with Rime. *Tiamat*'s logs: "FEATURE 7" and a 2171 timestamp. | FP | 2:00 |

### Chapter 7 — Contact (8 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 Confrontation | Sam and Mara confront Ashford in the drill chamber. Hana is already running the drill to collect a sample, under orders. Argument interrupted by the drill seizing. | FP-L | 1:00 |
| 7.2 **First full encounter** | The bore erupts with Rime. The Listener climbs out. Hana is pulled into the bore. Lights fail. | FP | 1:00 |
| 7.3 Retreat | Run, hide, shoot only when needed. Gunfire staggers but doesn't kill it. Ashford is wounded holding a pressure door. | FP | 2:00 |
| 7.4 Needles | Habitat vents start ticking. First Needle pair. "That's three." | FP | 1:30 |
| 7.5 Luca | Over the radio from the lander pad, Luca prepping *Wren* for evacuation, attacked mid-checklist. Gunfire, his breathing, then silence. *Wren* is destroyed. | FP (radio while moving) | 1:00 |
| 7.6 Ashford's confession | Trapped and dying behind a pressure door, Ashford tells Sam the truth and gives him ascent-vehicle codes. His channel cuts mid-sentence. "That's four." | FP-L | 1:30 |

### Chapter 8 — The Last Crew Member (6 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 Tunnels | Sam and Mara traverse the utility tunnels to the drill-chamber exit that leads to the ascent vehicle. Quiet. Their most honest conversation. | FP (co-op traversal) | 2:00 |
| 8.2 Drill chamber | The only route. The Listener waits, kneeling, palms on the floor. Stealth fails or succeeds; either way it ends in a fight. | FP | 2:00 |
| 8.3 **Mara's death** | See full sequence in [03-screenplay.md](03-screenplay.md#3-the-companions-death). | FP-L → CS (short) → FP-L | 1:30 |

### Chapter 9 — The Last Transmission (5 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1 Surface run | 600 m across the plain to the ascent vehicle. The Keel moves under the ice: ridges rise and fall. Boots-only audio. Hana's notebook (found in the drill chamber) teaches the node weakness. | FP | 2:00 |
| 9.2 Ascent | Launch alone using Ashford's codes. Mara's music player is clipped to her seat. | FP-L | 0:45 |
| 9.3 The sample **[CHOICE]** | Back on the *Meridian*: the cargo hold contains Ashford's sealed sample canister. It's pulsing at Feature 7's rhythm. Vent it, or keep it for Earth. | FP | 1:00 |
| 9.4 **The last transmission** | Sam records the mission's final message to Earth. Player chooses the closing line from three options (all in character). | FP-L | 1:15 |

### Chapter 10 — Home (3 min)
| Scene | Description | Control | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10.1 Splashdown (time card: "10 months later") | Capsule in the Pacific; recovery ship deck. Seagulls, wind, wet air: the first natural sound since Chapter 1. | FP (walk) | 1:00 |
| 10.2 Family | Ada and Zara. Zara asks whether Jupiter had a face. Sam hesitates. | FP-L | 1:00 |
