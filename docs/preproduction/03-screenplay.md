# 03 — Screenplay and Dialogue

**Line format.** Every spoken line specifies:

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | `IN PERSON` · `RADIO` (crew suit/ship channel) · `RECORDED` (delayed packet or log) · `PA` (facility speakers) |
| **Processing** | `none` · `R1` clean radio (band-pass 300–3400 Hz, light compression) · `R2` degraded (R1 + bit-crush, dropouts) · `R3` failing (R2 + heavy static, Feature 7 modulation) · `REC` recorded-packet (R1 + data-compression artefacts) · `HELM` inside-helmet (close-mic, mild resonance) |
| **Volume** | whisper · quiet · normal · raised · shout |
| **Timing** | trigger or timecode relative to the scene |

Delivery notes are written for actors, not readers. Pauses marked `(beat)` are ~1 s, `(long beat)` ~2–3 s. Overlaps are marked `//`.

## 3. The companion's death

**Location:** Drill chamber, Conamara Base. A circular room around the borehole, 20 m across, gantry above, one swinging work lamp, frost on everything. Exit door on the far side leads to the ascent-vehicle tunnel.

**Direction intent:** No jump scare. The audience should see the creature start to rise **before Mara does**, through Sam's eyes, and be unable to stop it. The violence is mostly *heard* and *framed*, not lingered on. One graphic moment, then the camera (Sam) looks at her face, not the wound.

## 4. Final escape and return to Earth

### 4.1 Chapter 9 — Surface run (no companion, boots-only audio)

The player crosses 600 m of plain to the ascent vehicle. The Keel moves under the ice: ridges rise in the player's path, the ground tilts, cracks open and close. Rime fields flicker if the player runs nearby, drawing a Listener-form (a second, smaller one). Hana's notebook (found next to the bore) has taught the player the **node rule**, so this time the player can kill it properly.

Radio wheel: **ASHFORD — no signal. MORI — no signal. FERREIRA — no signal. LINDQVIST — no signal.** Players who instinctively open a channel hear the error tone. **There is no dialogue in the surface run except Sam's breathing and two lines:**

### 4.2 Ascent

Seats for five. Sam sits in his. Mara's music player is clipped to her harness. If the player interacts, it plays 6 seconds of the song Luca used to tease her about, then the battery dies. Codes: 4117. Launch. Europa falls away; Jupiter swings across the window, enormous, indifferent.

### 4.3 The sample (choice)

In *Meridian*'s cargo hold: a sealed canister with Ashford's handwriting: **"FOR HOME."** Inside the frosted glass, a fist-sized piece of Rime pulses, slowly, at 0.4 Hz.

| # | Character | Line | Delivery | Vol. | Mode | Proc. | Timing |
